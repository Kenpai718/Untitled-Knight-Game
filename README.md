# TCSS491-Web-Game-Project: Untitled Knight Game
2D action platformer game where you play as a Knight.
Explore 2D castle themed levels and take down monsters who stand in your path.
You must save the day with your trusty sword, bow and parkour skills!
Explore the castle depths and loot the chests to upgrade your equipment at the shop!
Make your way though the dungeon and find out the source of this monster takeover!

**Play Here**: https://kenpai718.github.io/Untitled-Knight-Game/

**Game Trailer (v1.4)**: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vRtesQZ4C-o

If downloaded project use **'RUN.bat'** to start webgame
